What drives us

Mission, vision, values

Our mission

To make cross-border trade dependable. We run wholesale and retail across daily necessities, hardware products, household goods, electronic products, textiles and garments, and we make sure every order arrives as specified — and arrives on the date we promised.

Our vision

A Hong Kong trading house that operates like a technology company: one live system, one shared source of truth, and a mobile management app that puts every order, every container and every decision in front of the people who need it.

Our values

Specification first. Documents correct. Say the real lead time. Own the problem end to end. And keep the promise smaller than the one you can actually keep.

FOUNDATION

A Hong Kong trade desk

Zelin Trading Co., Limited starts from Tsim Sha Tsui with general trade and a single promise: tell the customer the real lead time before taking the order.

EXPANSION

Category by category

Wholesale and retail extend across daily necessities, hardware products, household goods, electronic products, textiles and garments — each with its own specification discipline.

OPERATIONS

Import, export and in-house documentation

Import and export of goods and technology becomes an owned function, with customs coordination and trade compliance handled by our own team rather than passed down the chain.

ADVISORY

Consulting and investment planning

Business information consultation and enterprise management advisory join the offering, alongside project investment planning for customers entering a new category.

SUPPLY CHAIN

Supporting supply chain services

Supporting supply chain services formalise the planning layer — buffer stock positions, supplier pipelines and consolidated shipping built on live inventory data.

DIGITAL

The mobile management app

Our mobile management application launches to the Google Play and Apple App Store, integrating real-time orders, inventory, supply chain tracking, consulting dashboards, investment planning and wholesale/retail management into one console.

Compliance & responsibility

Global trade, local rules

We operate general trade only, excluding regulated businesses such as finance, insurance and lending. Every shipment we handle is checked against the destination's import requirements before it is dispatched, and we keep the documentation trail for every consignment on file.

How we handle personal data — on this website and inside our mobile management app — is set out in full in our Privacy Policy. The rules that govern how you may use our website and app are in our Terms of Service.
