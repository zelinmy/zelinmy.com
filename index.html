One console, every business line

The mobile management app

Published to the Google Play and Apple App Store, our mobile management application brings every business line into a single interface — real-time orders, inventory, supply chain tracking, consulting dashboards, investment planning and wholesale/retail management, with the full scope of the business built in.

  • Real-time orders — every purchase order, allocation and shipment status in one live feed.
  • Inventory — stock levels across daily necessities, hardware, household goods, electronics and textiles.
  • Supply chain tracking — milestones from factory to door, including import and export documentation.
  • Consulting dashboard — business information consultation and enterprise management advisory reports.
  • Investment planning — project investment planning scenarios and category performance.
  • Wholesale & retail management — pricing tiers, account terms and margin visibility.
