1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Zelin Trading Co., Limited ("Zelin Trading", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit our website at zelinmy.com (the "Website"), contact us, or use our mobile management application published through the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store (the "App").

This Policy applies to the Website and the App together, and to our business operations described as: wholesale and retail of daily necessities, hardware products, household goods, electronic products, textiles and garments; import and export of goods and technology; business information consultation; supporting supply chain services; and general trade (excluding regulated businesses such as finance, insurance and lending).

Zelin Trading is the data controller for the personal information described in this Policy. Our service providers, including hosting providers, analytics providers and advertising technology partners, process information on our behalf as set out in section 5.

Your business counterparties, customers and suppliers may be individuals. Where you submit the personal information of another person (for example, a colleague on an order enquiry), you confirm that you have their permission to do so, and we will use that information only as described in this Policy.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide to us

  • Account and profile information. When you create an account in the App or submit an enquiry through the Website, we collect your name, business email address, company name, role, country or region, and any credentials you choose to set.
  • Enquiry and order information. The content of your enquiries and messages, purchase orders, product categories, quantities, destination details, delivery instructions and related correspondence.
  • Commercial records. Quotations, invoices, payment status, credit terms and account history, retained to the extent required for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Consulting materials. Where you engage our business information consultation, enterprise management advisory or project investment planning services, we collect the business information, market data and planning assumptions you or our advisors provide.
  • Support content. Screenshots, diagnostic logs or files you send us when troubleshooting an order or an issue in the App.

2.2 Information collected automatically from your device

  • Device information. Device model, operating system and version, app version, screen resolution, language and time zone settings, carrier and network type, and a device-generated identifier.
  • Usage information. Pages visited, screens viewed, features used, search and filter selections, timestamps of actions, in-app events, session duration and approximate location derived from your IP address (for example, a city or region level).
  • Advertising identifiers. The Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and the Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID), together with any other advertising or resettable device identifier available on your device.
  • Log information. IP address, browser or device type, request timestamps, referring page, crash reports and diagnostic events used to keep the service secure and stable.
  • Preferences. Your saved settings, language preference, notification preferences, and the categories, markets and suppliers you choose to follow in the App.

2.3 Optional location information

  • Coarse location. Your App settings may allow you to share approximate location. We use it only if you enable the feature, and only to show region-specific stock information and to pre-fill delivery destinations.
  • Precise location. We do not request precise GPS location. If your operating system prompts for precise location, declining it does not limit any feature of the App.

2.4 Information from third parties

  • Advertising and measurement partners. Advertising networks and measurement providers may share aggregated performance data, impression counts, campaign attribution and the advertising identifier they have associated with your device.
  • Trade counterparties. Where a customer or supplier's business record is legitimately shared with us as part of a transaction, we may receive limited related details.
  • Store platforms. Apple and Google provide basic app usage and crash analytics, and may provide a privacy-preserving identifier associated with your account.

2.5 Information we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data such as government identification numbers, payment card numbers (payment is handled by the store platform or a licensed payment processor), medical or biometric data, or information about your political opinions, religious beliefs or trade union membership. Please do not submit such information through the enquiry form.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the service. To operate your account, fulfil enquiries and orders, display live inventory and order status, run the mobile management features, and deliver the business information consultation, enterprise management advisory, project investment planning and supporting supply chain services you have requested.
  • To manage the trading relationship. To prepare quotations, process invoices, administer credit terms, handle returns and disputes, and maintain the commercial record of our dealings.
  • To support and improve the App and Website. To diagnose errors, monitor performance, understand which features are used, fix defects, and develop new functionality across our eight business lines.
  • To secure the service. To detect fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms of Service, and protect the security and integrity of our systems, our customers' data and our trade records.
  • To personalise advertising. To deliver advertising within the App that is more relevant to you, where permitted and where you have not opted out, and to measure the performance of that advertising.
  • To send service communications. Order confirmations, shipment updates, invoice notices, service announcements and responses to your enquiries. Marketing communications are sent only where permitted, and every message includes an unsubscribe control.
  • To meet legal obligations. To comply with tax, accounting, customs and trade compliance, record-keeping and regulatory requirements, and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • To act on your instructions. Including consent withdrawals, privacy choices and requests to delete or export your information.

8. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures proportionate to the nature of the information we hold, including:

  • TLS encryption for data in transit across the Website, the App and all administrative systems.
  • Encryption at rest for databases and backups, with managed key rotation.
  • Role-based access control, least-privilege permissions and multi-factor authentication for all staff access to customer data.
  • Environment separation between development, staging and production, with production access granted on a need-to-know basis.
  • Centralised logging and monitoring of access to personal data, with regular review.
  • Secure software development practices, dependency scanning and vulnerability patching on a defined schedule.
  • Periodic independent penetration testing and risk assessments of our systems and processes.
  • Vendor due diligence and written contractual obligations for every processor that handles personal data on our behalf.
  • Staff training on data protection, with confidentiality obligations in employment contracts.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within the period required by applicable law (for example, 72 hours under the GDPR), and notify affected individuals where the threshold is met. Where a breach affects users in Australia, Singapore, Brazil, Japan, Korea, Canada, India or the United States, we will make the notifications required by those regimes.

9.1 Personalised and non-personalised advertising

  • Personalised advertising. 9.2 GDPR, CCPA and consent management

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other consent-required regions, we present a consent platform before non-essential advertising is served. You may accept, reject or manage purposes individually. Rejecting consent does not limit your access to the App or its core features.

9.3 Apple App Tracking Transparency and advertising identifiers

  • On Apple devices running iOS 14 or later, the App presents the App Tracking Transparency prompt where tracking would otherwise occur. If you decline, we do not track you across apps and websites owned by other companies, and you receive contextual advertising only.
  • We request the IDFA only after you grant permission through the ATT prompt, and only where needed for advertising attribution.
  • We use the AAID (Google Advertising ID) only where it is permitted, we respect the device-level "Limit Ad Tracking" and "Delete" controls, and we reset identifiers when you ask us to.

13. Children's privacy and age policy

The Website and the App are business services intended for adults. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13, or under 16 where a higher threshold applies under local law.

  • COPPA (United States). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Where the legal basis for processing relies on consent and the individual is below the age of majority for consent in their jurisdiction, a parent or guardian must give consent. Our business services are not intended for children, so we do not knowingly seek that consent.
  • App store age ratings. The App is rated for a general audience and for ages 13 and over. We configure our advertising SDKs so that personalised advertising is not served to users known to be under the applicable threshold, and we disable data collection for known child-directed configurations where the platform provides the signal.

15. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

The Website does not respond to the legacy "Do Not Track" browser signal, because there is no widely adopted industry standard for interpreting it and because honouring it inconsistently would create a false impression of the protections you have.

We do honour the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal where it is recognised by law, including in California and other US states that require universal opt-out mechanism recognition. Where a valid GPC signal is detected, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we apply it across all advertising partners listed in section 9. Where a consent platform is presented, we do not serve non-essential advertising where the signal indicates refusal.
