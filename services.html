Category, volume, destination, target price and timing. For new accounts we run a business information consultation call first.

Questions

Before you ask

Which markets do you serve?
We ship from Hong Kong to customers across Asia, Europe, North America, the Middle East and Africa. Import and export of goods and technology is handled in-house, including documentation and customs coordination.
Is the mobile app required?
No — the app is optional for ordering, but it is where real-time orders, inventory, supply chain tracking, the consulting dashboard, investment planning and wholesale/retail management are surfaced. It is published to both the Google Play and Apple App Store.
What does project investment planning cover?
Category-level entry analysis, supplier mapping, margin modelling and scenario planning for wholesale and retail programmes, delivered as part of our business information consultation and enterprise management advisory work.
Do you hold buffer stock?
For daily necessities, household goods and electronic accessories we maintain buffer positions so repeat orders do not restart the lead-time clock. Stock levels are visible live in the app.
How are goods quality-checked?
Samples are approved before production, and inspections run against the signed specification before dispatch. For textiles and garments we also inspect at production stages, not only at the end.
Are regulated activities in scope?
No. Our activities cover general trade only, excluding regulated businesses such as finance, insurance and lending. If a request falls outside our scope we will tell you before taking it on.
